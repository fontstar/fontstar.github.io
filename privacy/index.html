Privacy Policy

FontStar does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

Because of how website styling works, FontStar requires permission to read and modify the webpages you visit in order to change their fonts. These permissions are used ONLY to analyze a page’s styles and replace them with your chosen fonts.

FontStar does NOT use these permissions to access or retain website content, track your browsing activity, or gather personal information. No data about the websites you visit is stored—either locally on your device or elsewhere.
