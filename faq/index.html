Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I use FontStar?

FontStar is exclusively designed for iPhone, iPad, and Mac (Apple Silicon). And it's not intended for all the websites, either. So I can't say you "should" use it. But here's why it's special:

  • Simple and beautiful
  • No ads, no tracking
  • Safe and privacy-respecting
  • Designed specifically for Apple devices

I designed this app because I use Safari every day and I'm tired of having to endure many website's poor readability and aesthetics. Apple devices are packed with so many beautiful fonts—why can't we just use them directly? So I decided to build an extension to do exactly that, after I scoured the App Store trying out all the apps out there, but couldn't find one that I truly love.

So far, I'm loving this one I've made, and I hope you'll love it too!
