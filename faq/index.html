Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I use FontStar?

FontStar is exclusively designed for iPhone, iPad, and Mac (Apple Silicon). And it’s not intended for all the websites, either. So I can’t say you “should” use it. But here’s why it’s special:

  • Simple and beautiful
  • No ads, no tracking
  • Safe and privacy-respecting
  • Designed specifically for Apple devices

I designed this app because I use Safari every day and I'm tired of having to endure many website's poor readability and aesthetics. Apple devices are packed with so many beautiful fonts—why can't we just use them directly? So I decided to build an extension to do exactly that, after I scoured the App Store trying out all the apps out there, but couldn't find one that I truly love.

So far, I'm loving this one I've made, and I hope you'll love it too!

Can I use FontStar in "Web Apps"?

Yes, starting with macOS 15 (Sequoia). FontStar works inside Web Apps created with Safari’s “Add to Dock” feature.

Why does the font I select appear as Times New Roman sometimes?

If you’re still experiencing this minor issue, please update your FontStar app to the latest version from the App Store.

Is FontStar safe?

Yes. FontStar and its Safari extension are completely safe to use. Your browsing data is never collected.

Is any of my browsing data collected?

No, FontStar does not collect, store, or transmit any data. The extension requires access to the webpages you visit in order to analyze their styles and replace fonts, but none of this data is ever stored.

Why does Apple’s alert say FontStar can “read everything”?

Apple’s alert text is intentionally broad to warn users about the potential misuse of extension permissions. While technically accurate, it does not reflect FontStar’s actual behavior.
